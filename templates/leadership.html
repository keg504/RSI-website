    Dr. Gnanaraj Jesudian

    Founder of RSI

    Biography

    Dr. Gnanaraj is a Urologist educated at Christian Medical College, Vellore. He has worked in multiple rural hospitals with organisations such as the Friends Missionary Prayer Band and Emmanuel Hospitals Associations, where he saw the need for innovation in rural surgery to make it more accessible. Based on his experiences, he has collaborated with universities internationally and local Indian medical equipment manufacturers to develop low-cost solutions for surgery in rural areas.

    He travels regularly to lead workshops in promoting and teaching surgery using the instruments he has helped develop, as well as to attend conferences where he can meet other professionals that can help develop more tools to make surgery accessible.

    Mr. Kesochie Chakhesang

    Founder of RSI

    Biography

    Mr. Kesochie Chakhesang is well-rounded manager with experience in qualifications in media, communications and economics from Nagaland, India. He has worked as a journalist where he developed his skills in media creation and editing. He has also worked as a press secretary, which has given him experience in public relations and liaising with the media.

    His business qualifications come from multiple businesses that he has been involved in at multiple levels, from being an administrator to being a manager of a hospital and a business owner for a company he started. His experience with business management is closely intertwined with work in healthcare organisations, and he consistently expands his skills and knowledge as he takes on more roles.

    Dr. Asif Gani

    Strategy Director

    Biography

    Dr. Asif Gani is a physician executive, orthopaedic surgeon and innovative medical strategist. He combines deep scientific understanding with a sharp grasp of brand and commercial needs, which lets him turn clinical insight into products and organisations that succeed in the real world. His expertise spans surgical care, healthcare systems leadership and international healthcare ventures. He has held leadership roles across hospitals, surgical centres, long term care and home healthcare, working at the intersection of patient care and enterprise transformation. He has strengthened his leadership and management expertise through education programmes from Harvard Medical School and the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland (RCSI).

    As Founder and Strategy Director of RSI, he leads the company’s strategic direction and growth. He brings together a surgeon’s understanding of the operating theatre and a strategist’s perspective on building sustainable, market ready healthcare enterprise. His focus is on forging partnerships with hospitals, governments and global health organisations, and on creating scalable models that take RSI’s frugal innovations and training programmes from India to low-resource settings worldwide, so that safe, affordable surgery reaches the patients who need it most, close to home.
